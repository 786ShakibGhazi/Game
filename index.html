Got questions?

Frequently asked questions

Is HEAD CRUSH really free?

Yes. It is free to play, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest. Sign in with Google or email if you want your progress saved and synced across devices.

Does the game upload my photos?

No. Face detection and storage happen on your device only. Photos are never sent to our servers.

How do I delete my account?

Open your Profile in the app and tap Delete account. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Which devices are supported?

Android 7.0 (Nougat) and newer. Camera is optional.
